Puzzly Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy covers the Puzzly mobile app (bundle ID com.gmail.vicapitallc.puzzly) and the Puzzly multiplayer puzzle service it talks to.

Data we collect

  • Account data. If you sign in with email, a guest session, or Sign in with Apple, authentication is handled by Clerk. We receive an account identifier and the profile details you provide, such as an email address.
  • Photos. If you create a puzzle from a photo, that image is uploaded so the puzzle can be built and played.
  • Gameplay data. Room membership, piece counts, piece placement, and related game state are stored so multiplayer rooms work.
  • Device data needed to run the app, such as a stored session token on your device.

How we use it

We use this data to create and sign in to your account, host puzzle rooms, show your photos as puzzles, and let you delete your account. We do not sell your data. The app’s privacy manifest sets tracking to off. We do not use your data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Who processes it

Clerk processes sign-in. Our servers (Express, Socket.IO, and Postgres) store account-linked game data and uploaded photos. Hosting may be on Replit or a later production host we name in the app.

How long we keep it

We keep account, photo, and gameplay data until you delete your account, or until we no longer need it to run the service.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile and use Delete account. You must confirm. That calls our delete-account endpoint for the signed-in user only and removes local account data and the Clerk identity.

Children

Puzzly is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

Email support at vicapitalllc@gmail.com. For account deletion, open Profile in the app and use Delete account.
